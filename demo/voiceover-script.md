## CircleSave-full-walkthrough.mp4 (2:34, to play inside the 3-minute pitch)

This is CircleSave. Around the world, more than a billion people save in rotating groups. They're called chamas in Kenya, susus in West Africa, and tandas in Latin America. Every week, everyone puts in money, and one person takes the whole pot home.

But one treasurer holds all the cash, the records live in a notebook, and years of faithful saving leave no record a bank can see. CircleSave replaces the treasurer with a smart contract on Stellar.

Here's how it works. The organizer sets the amount, the round length, and the payout order. Each member locks a deposit equal to one payment. Every round, everyone pays with one click in their wallet, and the contract sends the full pot to whoever's turn it is. If someone stops paying, their deposit covers them.

We built two AI agents with Claude, from Anthropic. The Circle Assistant reads the circle straight from the blockchain, using read-only tools. It answers questions and explains the rules, but it can never move money.

It can also write a friendly payment reminder in each member's own language, like Swahili, ready to send on WhatsApp.

To start a circle, the organizer enters the contribution, picks a round length, and lists the members by name, in payout order. The 3D preview shows the circle before it's created. Once it's created, nobody can change the rules.

This is a real circle, running on Stellar Testnet. Alice, Bob, and Carol each paid ten XLM per round, for three rounds. The 3D ring shows who has paid, and the contract paid the thirty XLM pot to each of them in turn.

Every member, their turn, and their deposit are visible to everyone. After the last round, the contract returned every deposit automatically.

Ask the assistant about the real record, and Claude reads the live contract to answer, by name.

Every on-time payment builds a portable good-saver score, stored on-chain. Alice has three out of three payments on time.

Our second agent, the Credit Analyst, turns that record into a plain-language report that a microfinance lender can read. That's the path from unbanked to banked.

Everything is public. Here's our contract on Stellar Expert, with every payment as a real transaction, each costing less than one cent in fees.

No treasurer to pay. Deposits instead of insurance. And lenders pay for the credit data. CircleSave: save together, build credit, and nobody holds the cash.
